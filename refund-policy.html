CUSTOMER POLICY

Refund, Return & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Thank you for choosing Jozibroc. We are committed to providing valuable digital products and a smooth customer experience.

Because our products are primarily delivered digitally and can be accessed immediately after purchase, we have specific policies regarding refunds, returns, and cancellations.

By purchasing from Jozibroc, you acknowledge and agree to the terms outlined below.

1. Digital Products

Jozibroc currently offers digital products, including but not limited to ebooks, guides, templates, workbooks, and other downloadable or electronically delivered resources.

Due to the nature of digital products, all digital product sales are generally final once the product has been successfully delivered or access has been provided.

Once a digital product has been downloaded, accessed, or delivered to the email address or account provided at checkout, it cannot be physically returned.

2. Refund Policy

We do not generally offer refunds for digital products simply because a customer:

  • Changes their mind after purchase;
  • No longer wants the product;
  • Purchased the wrong product;
  • Did not read the product description before purchasing;
  • Decides the product is not suitable for their personal needs;
  • Fails to download the product within the available access period; or
  • Has already downloaded or accessed the product.

We strongly encourage customers to carefully review the product description, features, format, and other available information before completing a purchase.

Exceptional Refund Circumstances

A refund may be considered where:

  • You were charged more than once for the same product due to a technical or payment error;
  • You paid for a product but did not receive access because of a technical issue on our end;
  • The purchased digital product is materially different from the product description;
  • The file is defective or corrupted and we are unable to provide a functional replacement; or
  • There was an error attributable to Jozibroc that prevented you from receiving the product you purchased.

Refund requests will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

3. Refund Request Period

If you experience a problem with your purchase, please contact us within 7 days of your purchase.

When contacting us, please provide:

  • Your full name;
  • The email address used for the purchase;
  • Your order or transaction reference;
  • The name of the product purchased; and
  • A clear description of the issue.

We may request additional information reasonably necessary to verify the purchase and investigate the issue.

Submitting a refund request does not automatically guarantee that a refund will be approved.

4. Duplicate Payments

If you believe you have been charged more than once for the same product, please contact us as soon as possible.

After verifying the duplicate transaction, we will take appropriate steps to resolve the issue, which may include issuing a refund for the duplicate payment.

5. Cancellation Policy

Before Delivery

If you contact us immediately after placing an order and the digital product has not yet been delivered or access has not yet been provided, we may be able to cancel the order.

However, cancellation is not guaranteed once payment has been successfully processed and the product has been automatically delivered or access has been granted.

After Delivery or Access

Once a digital product has been delivered, downloaded, or accessed, the order generally cannot be cancelled or reversed.

This is because digital products cannot be returned in the same way as physical goods.

6. Failed or Unsuccessful Transactions

If your payment was unsuccessful or your account was debited but you did not receive your product, please contact us.

We will verify the transaction and, where payment has been successfully received, work to provide the purchased product or resolve the payment issue.

Please do not make multiple payments while waiting for a previous transaction to be confirmed unless instructed to do so.

7. Technical Problems

If you experience difficulties downloading, accessing, or opening a purchased digital product, please contact us before requesting a refund.

Where reasonably possible, we will first attempt to resolve the technical issue or provide a replacement copy of the product.

Technical difficulties caused by a customer's device, internet connection, software, email provider, or other third-party service may not qualify for a refund if the product itself is functioning correctly.

8. Incorrect Email Address or Customer Information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate information during checkout.

If you provide an incorrect email address or other incorrect delivery information, please contact us as soon as possible.

We will make reasonable efforts to assist you, but Jozibroc cannot guarantee recovery or re-delivery where incorrect information was supplied by the customer.

9. Promotional Offers and Discounts

Products purchased using promotional codes, discounts, special offers, bundles, or limited-time promotions remain subject to this Refund/Return and Cancellation Policy unless otherwise stated at the time of purchase.

A promotional discount does not create a separate right to a refund or cancellation.

10. Product Satisfaction

We want you to get meaningful value from your purchase.

Before purchasing, please take time to review the product description and any available previews or information about what is included.

If you have questions about a product before purchasing, you are encouraged to contact us so that we can help you determine whether it is suitable for your needs.

11. Chargebacks and Payment Disputes

If you experience a problem with your purchase, we encourage you to contact Jozibroc first so that we can attempt to resolve the issue.

Unresolved payment disputes or chargebacks may be investigated using the transaction and purchase information associated with the order.

This does not limit any rights you may have under applicable consumer protection or payment laws.

12. How to Contact Us

For questions regarding refunds, returns, cancellations, failed payments, or technical issues relating to your purchase, please contact us through the contact details provided on the Jozibroc website.

When contacting us, please include your order or transaction reference where available so that we can assist you more efficiently.

13. Changes to This Policy

Jozibroc reserves the right to update or modify this policy when necessary.

Any changes will be published on this page and will become effective from the date stated at the top of the policy.

By completing a purchase on the Jozibroc website, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Refund, Return & Cancellation Policy.
