ABOUT JOZIBROC

Useful systems, without the unnecessary complexity.

Elizabeth Sambo started Jozibroc after years of doing the unglamorous work of writing letters, drafting contracts, and organising records for others.

She realised many Nigerian women need the same practical tools — just packaged so they don't have to start from a blank page.

Every guide is written from lived experience, tested with real users, and priced for the market it's made for.
