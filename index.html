ABOUT JOZIBROC

Useful systems.
No unnecessary
complexity.

Elizabeth Sambo started Jozibroc after years of doing the unglamorous work of writing letters, drafting contracts and organising records for others.

She realised that many Nigerian women need the same practical tools — just packaged so they don't have to start from a blank page.

Every guide sold on Jozibroc is written from lived experience, tested with real users and priced for the market it is made for.

No jargon. No filler. Just the exact system, ready to use.
